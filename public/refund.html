Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2024

Deposits

All project deposits are non-refundable. This covers initial planning, research, and resource allocation.

Project Cancellations

If a project is cancelled after work has begun, you are responsible for payment of all completed work up to the cancellation date.

Hourly Services

Consulting and support hours are billed in 15-minute increments. Unused hours expire 30 days from purchase.

Mistakes and Corrections

We will correct any errors in delivered work at no charge within 30 days of delivery. After 30 days, corrections are billed at our standard rate.

Refunds for Unused Hours

For prepaid monthly support plans, we offer prorated refunds for unused hours if you cancel before the next billing cycle.
